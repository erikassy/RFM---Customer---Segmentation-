# RFM-Customer-Segmentation
RFM Analysis &amp; Customer Segmentation using Power BI
RFM ANALYSIS & CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION
PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project is about a retail business which provides variety of products for customers from different regions. It analyzes customer purchasing behaviour using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to segment customers based on their engagement and overall value to the business.
The analysis was developed to identify high value customers, customers at risk of churning and other customer segments that can support targeted retention and marketing campaigns
BUSINESS PROBLEM
The business has transaction level customer data but lacks a clear understanding of which customers are most valuable, which customers are highly engaged and which may be at risk of becoming inactive customers. Without proper customer segmentation, the efforts of marketing and retention may not be effectively targeted.
Key Business Questions
i.	Who are the most valuable customers?
ii.	Which segments generate the most revenue?
iii.	Which customers purchase most frequently?
iv.	Which customers should the business prioritize for retention
v.	Which segment purchased product category with the highest revenue
BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
This business has a big picture and below are the main objectives used in this analysis
a.	Identify high-value customers
b.	Detect loyal customers
c.	Identify at-risk customers
d.	Improve retention strategy
e.	Support targeted marketing campaigns
DATASET
Data Source
The analysis was conducted using a transactional sales dataset containing customer purchase records. It is a Kaggle dataset, Applied Data Analytics Training Centre.

Dataset Structure
The raw dataset contains approximately 30,000 transaction records which includes;
a.	100 unique customers
b.	5 regions
c.	4 Product category
Columns	Description
Transaction_ID	Sales Transaction ID
Customer ID	Unique Customer Identifier
Region	Customer Region
Date	Transaction date
Product_ID	Product code
Product_Name	Product Name
Product_Category	Category
Quantity	Units Purchased
PPU	Price Per Unit
Amount	Transaction total amount
